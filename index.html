Mumbai · in practice since 2023

"What I really bring is a deep understanding of what it feels like to carry guilt, success, shame, and silence in the same body."

I hold a Master's in Psychology (Clinical) from KC College, Mumbai, and I'm trained to work with trauma, grief, life transitions, and stress. Across 2000+ hours of therapy and 100+ clients, the work I keep coming back to is the same: helping people who look like they have it all figured out finally exhale.

Maybe you're the one everyone leans on. The one who shows up, delivers, holds the room together — and still feels the same knot underneath, no matter how much you've achieved or how hard you've tried to think your way out of it.

That gap between how put-together you seem and how tired you actually are — that's exactly what we work with here.
